  1. Choose your items. Browse the shop and add what you want to your cart. Physical items show how many are in stock.
  2. Log in or create an account. You need an account to check out, so you can see your orders and downloads later.
  3. Check out. Enter your name, email and phone number. If your order includes physical items, add your delivery address and city. You can download a PDF quote before you pay.
  4. Pay. Pay the total by bank transfer or EcoCash using the details below, then enter your payment reference and upload your proof of payment (an image or PDF, up to 4 MB).
  5. We confirm your payment. A BCU administrator checks it and updates your order. You will receive an email, and you can follow the status under My orders.
  6. Receive your items. Digital items (sermons, resources) unlock on your order page once payment is confirmed. See delivery and downloads for physical items.
